How to play
  1. One player creates a game and picks a start & target article (or rolls random). They get a 5-character room code.
  2. Others join — either with the room code (cross-device) or automatically in another tab on the same browser.
  3. The host starts the race. Everyone lands on the start article.
  4. Click only blue links inside articles to navigate. Each click = +1 to your score.
  5. First player to reach the target article wins. Lowest click count breaks ties.
